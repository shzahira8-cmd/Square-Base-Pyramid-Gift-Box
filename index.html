6.0 MATHEMATICAL CALCULATIONS

Given: side length of square base, s = 5 cm; perpendicular height, h = 6 cm; measured slant height, l = 6.5 cm.

6.1 VERIFICATION OF SLANT HEIGHT

Slant height (l) is the distance from the apex down the centre of a triangular face to the midpoint of a base edge. It can be calculated using the Pythagorean theorem.

Formula: l = √[h² + (s/2)²]

Where:

• l = slant height

• h = vertical height = 6 cm

• s = side of base length = 5 cm

Substitute the values into the formula:

l = √[6² + (5/2)²]

l² = 36 + (2.5)²

l² = 36 + 6.25

l² = 42.25

l = √42.25

l = 6.5 cm

Thus, the calculated slant height is 6.5 cm, which matches the measurement used in the gift box design.

6.2 CALCULATION OF THE BASE AREA

The base of the pyramid is a square with a side length of 5 cm.

Formula: B = s²

Where s = side of base length = 5 cm.

Substitute the values into the formula:

B = 5²

B = 25 cm²

Thus, the area of the square base is 25 cm².

6.3 CALCULATION OF LATERAL SURFACE AREA

The square pyramid consists of four identical triangular faces.

Formula: LSA = 2sl

Where:

• s = side of base length = 5 cm

• l = slant height = 6.5 cm

Substitute the values into the formula:

LSA = 2(5)(6.5)

LSA = 2(32.5)

LSA = 65 cm²

Thus, the total lateral surface area is 65 cm².

6.4 CALCULATION OF TOTAL SURFACE AREA

The total surface area includes the square base and the four triangular faces.

Formula: TSA = Base Area + Lateral Surface Area

TSA = 25 + 65

TSA = 90 cm²

Thus, the total surface area of the square pyramid gift box is 90 cm². This calculation covers the pyramid's square base and four triangular faces; it does not include any extra glue tabs or construction flaps.

6.5 CALCULATION OF VOLUME

Formula: V = ⅓s²h

Where:

• h = vertical height = 6 cm

• s = side of base length = 5 cm

Substitute the values into the formula:

V = ⅓(5²)(6)

V = ⅓(25)(6)

V = ⅓(150)

V = 50 cm³

Thus, based on the formula, the volume of the square pyramid gift box is 50 cm³. This represents its theoretical internal capacity before allowing for cardboard thickness, folds or glue tabs.

Note: Surface area is measured in square centimetres (cm²), while volume is measured in cubic centimetres (cm³). The slant height is measured along a triangular face, while the perpendicular height is measured vertically from the apex to the centre of the square base.
